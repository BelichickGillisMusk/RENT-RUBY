Owner Safeguards

Protect the owner before there is a dispute.

The portal is not just convenient for tenants. It creates evidence: timestamps, read receipts, signature records, notice history, sublease acknowledgments, and offsite backups.

Automatic Legal Notices
Notice to Enter, lease updates, and construction notices.

The portal can generate proper 94609 notice packets, deliver them consistently, and store the exact notice version sent to Unit 105.

Timestamped Read Receipts
Sent, opened, viewed, acknowledged.

Every tenant-facing notice can carry a timestamped audit trail: delivery time, first open, acknowledgment, IP/device metadata, and the document hash.

Signature Evidence
Tenant signature with domain/location metadata.

Lease updates and disclosures can record signer name, unit, timestamp, IP/domain, device, and geolocation approximation for owner defense.

Sublease Protection
Proof they read occupancy and guest rules.

Resident acknowledgments can document sublease restrictions, authorized occupants, guest policy reminders, and evidence against later false claims.

Offsite Archive
Saved outside the device and building.

Notices, signatures, payment evidence, and maintenance records can be mirrored to offsite storage so records survive device loss, staff turnover, or local machine failure.

False-Claim Defense
Clear timeline beats memory.

If a tenant says they never saw a notice, never signed, or were not told about construction, the owner has a clean event timeline and document history.
